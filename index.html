Security

Private by design. Nothing readable leaves your phone.

Family documents are some of the most sensitive data you own, so security was built in from day one.

1

Only you can open it

Google sign-in, then your own app PIN or fingerprint. DocSteep locks itself when you leave.

2

Encrypted on your phone

Every document, file and record is protected with AES-256 encryption, and the keys are kept in your phone's secure hardware.

3

Encrypted in your own Drive

Backups are encrypted again before upload to your own Google Drive. Only your account can open them.

4

You stay in control

Mark each document Public, Shareable or Private. Sharing needs your fingerprint, and private documents can't be shared at all.

🔒 Screenshots blocked 🧹 Sign-out wipes the phone 📍 No tracking 🗑️ Delete data anytime ☁️ Restore on a new phone
How to use

Up and running in minutes

Four simple steps to get your family's paperwork under control.

Install & sign in

Search DocSteep on the Indus Appstore and install it. Pick your language, sign in with Google and set your app PIN.

Add your family

In People, add each member with their life stage. DocSteep builds a checklist for each person.

Capture documents

Tap ➕ Add, then scan, take a photo or pick a file. Choose the person, category, expiry date and privacy level.

Stay on top

Check Home each day and watch for the daily summary. Use Search to find anything in seconds.
